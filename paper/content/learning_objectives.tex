\section{Learning Objectives}

Upon completion of this project, I sought to:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Develop an understanding of compressed sensing, demonstrate its application in ECG signals.
    \item Analyze different wavelet types and their efficacy in generating sparsity in ECG signals.
    \item Compare minimization types for reconstructing the ECG signals, specifically \(\ell_1\)-norm, \(\ell_{2,1}\) mixed-norm, and other techniques.
    \item Summarize modern literature for ECG compressed sensing to evaluate alternate approaches.
\end{itemize}
